## About

I'm **Gideon Oreoluwa Godwin**, also known as **Monger Gideon** — a software engineer focused on building complete digital products across **mobile, web, backend systems, real-time infrastructure, fintech integrations, and cloud deployment**.

I work across the full product lifecycle:

`Architecture` → `UI & Frontend` → `Backend & APIs` → `Database` → `Integrations` → `Infrastructure` → `Deployment`

I operate through **Monger Gideon Tech**, where I work with startups, businesses, and individuals to turn ideas into reliable, production-ready products.

My strongest advantage is end-to-end execution: I can design the architecture, build the application, configure the server, integrate payments and third-party services, deploy to production, and maintain the system after launch.

## How I Work

```text
01  DISCOVER
    Understand the product, users, constraints, and business goal.

02  ARCHITECT
    Plan the database, API structure, application flow, infrastructure,
    integrations, and deployment strategy before implementation.

03  BUILD
    Develop in clear milestones with reusable architecture and
    production-quality engineering practices.

04  INTEGRATE
    Connect payments, identity services, maps, notifications,
    storage, communication tools, and external APIs.

05  DEPLOY
    Configure infrastructure, CI/CD, DNS, SSL, servers,
    monitoring, and app-store releases.

06  SUPPORT
    Maintain, optimise, debug, and scale the product after launch.
```
